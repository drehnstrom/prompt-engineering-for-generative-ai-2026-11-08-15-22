# Course Title: Prompt Engineering for Generative AI (1 hour)

## Course Description

Generative AI models can produce remarkable answers, code, and creative content, but vague or poorly structured prompts often yield inaccurate, generic, or hallucinated responses. Without foundational prompt engineering skills, users struggle to unlock the full potential of language models and waste time on trial and error.

This course teaches beginners how to design structured, clear, and context-rich prompts that consistently deliver high-quality results. Through targeted lessons and a hands-on lab in Google Gemini, students master key prompt patterns, role prompting, few-shot examples, and iterative refinement.

## Learning Objectives

- Construct clear and effective prompts to achieve high-quality results from generative AI
- Apply structured prompt components, context framing, and iterative refinement techniques in Gemini

## Who Should Attend

Beginners, professionals, and knowledge workers who want to communicate effectively with generative AI tools to improve daily productivity and output quality.

## Prerequisites

- Basic familiarity with generative AI concepts or tools

## Hands-On Activities

- Lab: Engineering Effective Prompts in Gemini

## Course Outline

**Module 1: Prompt Engineering Essentials**

- Introduction to Prompt Engineering
- Core Elements of a Prompt: Role, Task, Context, and Constraints
- Direct Instructions and Formatting Output
- Few-Shot Prompting with Examples
- Avoiding Ambiguity and Mitigating Hallucinations
- Iterative Prompt Refinement
- Lab: Engineering Effective Prompts in Gemini
