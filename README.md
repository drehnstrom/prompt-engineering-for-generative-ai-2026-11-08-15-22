# Prompt Engineering for Generative AI (1 hour)

Generative AI models can produce remarkable answers, code, and creative content, but vague or poorly structured prompts often yield inaccurate, generic, or hallucinated responses. Without foundational prompt engineering skills, users struggle to unlock the full potential of language models and waste time on trial and error.

This course teaches beginners how to design structured, clear, and context-rich prompts that consistently deliver high-quality results. Through targeted lessons and a hands-on lab in Google Gemini, students master key prompt patterns, role prompting, few-shot examples, and iterative refinement.
